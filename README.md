In my workbook, I calculated profit margin by hand by dividing total profit by total sales, which gave one fixed percentage that only changed when I rebuilt it. In Power BI, I would build it as a measure, Profit Margin Ratio = DIVIDE([TotalProfit],[TotalSales]), rather than a calculated column. A column would calculate a margin for each individual order, and averaging those gives you the wrong answer for a region or product line, while a measure recalculates the ratio from the totals of whatever you have filtered. Because it lives in the model, the number stays accurate as new sales data comes in, without anyone needing to update the formula. That means you, Anita, and Marcus can click on any region, product, or quarter during a meeting and see the real margin right away, instead of waiting on me for an updated spreadsheet that may already be out of date.
